Built for the whole path, not just the stopwatch

Most tools in this space are SaaS products that keep your people’s hours, rates and invoices on someone else’s servers. Sheet Happens covers time entry to paid invoice and runs where you run it.

  • Immutable timesheet revisions

    Submitting a month snapshots its entries and resolved rates and locks the period. Approved numbers never recalculate from live data; a correction is a new revision, and older ones stay for audit.

  • Contractor invoice verification

    Contractors upload invoice PDFs against approved hours. Admins review them next to the timesheet snapshot and accept or reject with a reason, up to paid. Sheet Happens never issues invoices itself.

  • Fast time tracking

    Weekly grid, entry form and a running timer, with descriptions recalled from earlier entries. A 24-hour daily cap and locked periods are enforced on the server. Or declare hours per project per month with a proof PDF.

  • Reports and PDF export

    Reports by date range, project, project group and member. PDFs are rendered with Typst, as a cost-free version (hours only) or an internal one with rates and costs.

  • Invite-only and secure

    Admin and member roles, employee and contractor profiles. Sign in with a magic link, a passkey or a password, with authenticator-app 2FA, optional Google Workspace sign-in and an audit log of business changes.

  • Works with AI agents

    A built-in MCP server protected by OAuth 2.1 lets Claude and other MCP clients read and log time as the signed-in member, under the same period locks, daily cap and audit log as the web app.

Self-host it

One Docker image, your Postgres, your S3-compatible storage. One company per installation, and no built-in telemetry.

  1. Prepare the basics

    PostgreSQL 17 or 18, an S3-compatible bucket, an SMTP server for sign-in links, and a domain with HTTPS.

  2. Build and configure

    Build the image from the included Dockerfile and set the environment variables. The guide has a ready Docker Compose example, and a Railway route too.

  3. Migrate and start

    Apply the database migrations, start the app and open the first admin invitation. Your own organisation name and logo are a setting away.
