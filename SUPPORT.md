# Getting help

## Community support

- **Questions** about running or using Sheet Happens: ask in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/rolloutit/sheet-happens/discussions).
- **Bugs** and **feature requests**: open an [issue](https://github.com/rolloutit/sheet-happens/issues/new/choose).
  Search the existing ones first.
- **Security problems**: do not use either of the above, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Community support is best-effort and answered by maintainers and other users in their spare time.

## Professional support

The code is open and the self-hosted version is not crippled: there is no paid edition with extra
features. [Rollout IT](https://rolloutit.net), the company that builds and maintains Sheet Happens,
earns its living from support, installation and hosting, and from custom development for teams that
need something specific. If you only need an answer, ask in Discussions. If you need a person on the
hook, talk to us at [rolloutit.net](https://rolloutit.net).
